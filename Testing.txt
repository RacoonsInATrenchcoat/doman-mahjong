Round 2:

FEATURE REQUEST:

yakuman score calculation is wrong because it's using han
for instance: Honitsu (3 han) + Chinitsu (6 han) + Iipeikou (1 han) + Toitoi (2 han) + Sanankou (2 han) + Suuankou (13 han) = 27 han
the actual value of this hand is just suuankou, because that yaku is a yakuman. once you reach yakuman status (either by obtaining a yakuman itself or by getting to 13 han through regular yaku (counted yakuman)), the rest of the hand is not counted
you can only upgrade a yakuman if you get more yakuman alongside it, turning it into a double, triple, etc... yakuman. for instance, if you get suuankou with all honours (tsuuisou), that hand is worth a double yakuman (equivalent of 24 han)
note that counted yakuman cannot be upgraded, even if you somehow manage to get 24 han in a hand without a yakuman (eg. dora), it's still just 1 yakuman (= 13 han)
daisuushi is counted as a yakuman when in reality its a double yakuman (because it includes shousuushi which is also a yakuman, but they are mutually exclusive when calculating the score)

fu calculation should be visible together with the han value, because with less than 5 han that impacts the actual final score
